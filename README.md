### 🧠 NextHire AI — AI-Powered Job Portal

A full-stack platform that connects **job seekers and recruiters**, with an AI layer that reads resumes and actually tells you what's wrong with them.

**What it does**
- 🔐 Secure **JWT authentication** with role-based access for candidates & recruiters
- 🤖 **Gemini AI** analyzes resumes → generates **ATS scores**, flags **skill gaps**, and gives personalized career recommendations
- 📊 Separate **recruiter & candidate dashboards** — job posting, application tracking, profile management
- 🎯 **AI-powered job matching** between candidate profiles and open roles
- ⚡ RESTful APIs on **Express.js**, data modeled and managed in **MongoDB**
- 📱 Fully responsive **React + Tailwind** frontend

`React.js` `Node.js` `Express.js` `MongoDB` `REST APIs` `JWT` `Tailwind CSS` `Gemini AI`
